# Miu TOEIC

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js, Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Vercel.

## Users
Vietnamese TOEIC learners use Google sign-in to study vocabulary and grammar. One administrator imports and manages learning content.

## Product Purpose
Make short daily vocabulary and grammar practice simple, with progress retained per learner.

## Operating Context
The administrator prepares UTF-8 CSV files. Learners mostly use phones and want quick feedback after each grammar answer.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Topic flashcards, grammar theory and four-choice quiz, learner progress and history, CSV preview and atomic import, archival rather than hard deletion. Google login. Only the verified configured administrator email can manage content.

## Brand Commitments
Cute notebook-and-sticker aesthetic with a kitten mascot called Miu; learning content must remain legible and calm.

## Product Principles
- A learner can resume within one tap.
- Feedback is immediate and specific.
- Content management is safe and reversible.
